## Project Overview

This project presents a comprehensive business valuation case study for Asterion Industrial Technologies, a fictional industrial technology and precision-components company.

The analysis combines discounted cash flow (DCF) valuation, weighted average cost of capital (WACC), trading comparable company analysis, financial forecasting and sensitivity analysis.

The objective is to demonstrate the application of professional valuation methodologies to support investment evaluation, M&A transactions, corporate finance and strategic decision-making.

All financial information, company names, valuation assumptions and comparable-company data are synthetic and illustrative.

## Valuation Methodologies

### 1. Discounted Cash Flow (DCF) — Perpetual Growth

The DCF analysis estimates enterprise value by discounting projected unlevered free cash flows and terminal value using the company's WACC.

Key components include:

- Five-year financial forecasts
- Revenue and EBITDA projections
- Capital expenditure and working capital assumptions
- Unlevered free cash flow calculations
- WACC-based discounting
- Terminal value using the perpetual growth methodology

### 2. DCF — Exit Multiple

An alternative terminal value approach applies an illustrative exit EV/EBITDA multiple to the final forecast year's EBITDA.

This provides an additional valuation reference point and facilitates comparison with the perpetual growth DCF methodology.

### 3. Trading Comparable Companies

The model includes an illustrative peer-group analysis based on fictional industrial technology companies.

Valuation considerations include:

- Enterprise value and equity value
- Revenue and EBITDA multiples
- EV/Revenue and EV/EBITDA
- Peer-group valuation benchmarking
- Implied enterprise and equity valuation

### 4. Weighted Average Cost of Capital (WACC)

The cost of capital analysis incorporates:

- Risk-free rate
- Equity market risk premium
- Equity beta
- Cost of equity
- Pre-tax and after-tax cost of debt
- Target debt and equity capital structure
- Weighted average cost of capital

### 5. Sensitivity Analysis

The model evaluates the sensitivity of valuation conclusions to changes in:

- WACC
- Perpetual growth assumptions
- Terminal valuation assumptions

## Illustrative Valuation Results

| Valuation Method | Enterprise Value | Equity Value |
|---|---:|---:|
| DCF — Perpetual Growth | $719.54m | $596.54m |
| DCF — Exit Multiple | $790.69m | $667.69m |
| Trading Comparables | $607.44m | $484.44m |

**Key Illustrative Assumptions:**

- WACC: 8.96%
- Perpetual growth rate: 2.50%
- Exit EV/EBITDA multiple: 9.0x

Approximately 78.7% of the perpetual-growth DCF enterprise value is attributable to terminal value, highlighting the importance of long-term assumptions and sensitivity analysis.

These results are illustrative model outputs, not estimates of the market value of an actual business.
